Project 2: Wind Mechanic Prototype

Technologies: Unity 6, C#, WebGL

Description: A 3D sailing prototype that explores a custom wind drawing mechanic. Players navigate a tutorial level by drawing screen space wind gusts to control a ship's momentum and steering. Built to iterate on playtest feedback regarding physics tuning and intuitive UI.

My Contributions: Designed and programmed the core mechanic, developed the 3-zone tutorial level, implemented 3D line rendering math, and tuned the Rigidbody physics.
